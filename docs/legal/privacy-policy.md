# RevoCred Privacy Policy

Effective date: [[EFFECTIVE DATE]]

RevoCred is operated by [[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]] ("RevoCred", "we", "us"), [[MAILING ADDRESS]]. Questions or requests: [support@revocred.com](mailto:support@revocred.com).

This policy explains what the RevoCred app and service collect, why, who receives it and the choices you have. RevoCred is available only to people aged 18 or older in the United States.

## Summary

- You enter your own information. We never pull your credit report, connect to your bank or ask for card numbers.
- Card recommendations are calculated by our own server with fixed rules. AI never chooses or scores cards.
- REVO, our assistant, sends your question and plan details to an outside AI service only after you agree, and only if you have an account.
- You can export your data or delete your account in the app at any time.
- We do not sell your personal information, show third-party ads or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

## Information we collect

- **Account:** email address, password (stored by our authentication provider, never by us in readable form), account ID, sign-in sessions, an optional display name, and whether you opted in to update emails.
- **Questionnaire and plan:** life stage, credit band you select, monthly spending by category, reward goals, annual-fee and travel preferences, and the recommendations and strategy we calculate for you.
- **Wallet:** the cards you add, including custom cards and terms you type in.
- **REVO chat:** your questions, REVO's answers, any feedback or reports you send about an answer, and how many AI answers you have used each day.
- **Subscription status:** if you subscribe to REVO AI, whether your subscription is active. Apple handles payment; we never receive your payment card details.
- **Diagnostics:** error categories reported by the app (not message text or stack traces), and request metadata such as IP address, time, path and response status that our servers and hosting provider process to run and secure the service.
- **Apply link counts:** when you open an Apply link we count opens per offer, day and screen. These counts do not include your account ID, cookies, IP address or device identifiers.

Do not enter card numbers, security codes, PINs, bank passwords or Social Security numbers anywhere in the app, including REVO chat.

## How we use information

We use your information to create and secure your account, calculate and save your recommendations and strategy, answer REVO questions, send account emails (confirmation, password reset, email change), send optional update emails if you opted in, manage REVO AI subscriptions and daily limits, prevent abuse, and fix problems. We do not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking, and we do not sell or share it for those purposes.

## REVO and outside AI

REVO can answer in two ways:

- **Basic answers** come from RevoCred's own server. No outside AI service is involved. Guests (people who are not signed in) always get basic answers.
- **AI answers** use an outside AI service. Before your first AI answer, the app shows a sharing notice naming the service and the information it receives, and asks for your permission. Choose Cancel to send nothing, or Basic answer instead to keep using our server only.

If you agree, each AI request contains your question and relevant plan context: your first name, life stage, credit band, spending, wallet, strategy, card ranking and up to eight recent REVO messages. Your permission is saved for your account until you turn it off in Profile → AI answers (REVO). We ask again whenever the AI service or the sharing notice changes.

Our AI service is **OpenAI**. Under OpenAI's API data terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train OpenAI's models by default and may be kept for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. When OpenAI is in use, messages and answers also pass through OpenAI's moderation check to filter harmful content. During pre-release testing we may instead use **OpenCode** (OpenCode Go and the model provider it routes to, such as DeepSeek), which also receives an account-linked session identifier. The sharing notice in the app always names the service actually in use.

AI answers are for education only and are not financial, tax, credit or legal advice. They can be wrong or out of date. They never change your recommendations or scores.

## Web lookups

To check current card terms, REVO may search the web and read public web pages and PDFs. When web search is enabled, search queries go to **Brave Search**. Search queries contain public product or topic words only, not your profile or chat history, and our server blocks obvious personal identifiers in these requests. Public pages are fetched by our server, so those sites see our server, not your device. Public sources may be incomplete or outdated.

## Who receives information

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run RevoCred, and only as needed for that work:

| Provider | Purpose | Information |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Sign-in and our database | Account, plan, wallet, chat and all other account data |
| Railway | Hosting for our API server | All data passing through the API, request metadata |
| Resend | Sending account emails | Email address and email content |
| OpenAI (or OpenCode during testing) | AI answers and moderation, only after you agree | Question and plan context described above |
| Brave Search | Web search, when enabled | Public product or topic queries |
| Apple and RevenueCat | REVO AI subscriptions | Purchase and subscription status, an app user ID |
| Our email provider | Replying to support requests | What you send us |

Every third party that receives personal information from us must protect it with the same or equal protection as described in this privacy policy, use it only to provide services to us, and keep it confidential.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of users or the public, or as part of a merger or sale of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

Apply links open the card issuer's or a partner's website in your browser. We do not send your wallet, spending or chat to those sites. Their own privacy policies apply. We may earn a commission from labeled partner links; this never affects your recommendations.

Card information may come from OpenCard DB and issuer websites. Downloading that public data does not send any of your information.

## Retention

- Your account data is kept while your account is open.
- REVO keeps your latest 1,000 chat messages and up to 100 recommendation runs; older ones are deleted automatically as new ones arrive.
- When you delete your account, we delete your sign-in account and your profile, questionnaire, wallet, recommendations, chat, feedback and AI consent records from our live database right away.
- Copies in our database backups are deleted when those backups expire, within 7 days.
- AI and search providers keep data under their own terms, as described above.
- On your device, the app stores your sign-in session and appearance setting. A guest questionnaire draft expires after 24 hours.

## Your choices and rights

- **Export:** Profile → Account settings → Export my data downloads your data as a JSON file.
- **Delete:** Profile → Account settings → Delete my account permanently deletes your account. You will be asked for your password. Files you exported or shared are not recalled.
- **AI permission:** turn off Profile → AI answers (REVO) at any time. You will get basic answers until you agree again.
- **Update emails:** unsubscribe using the link in any update email, or email us.
- **Subscription:** manage or cancel REVO AI in your iPhone's Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.
- **Requests:** email [support@revocred.com](mailto:support@revocred.com) to access, correct or delete your information, or if you cannot sign in. Depending on your state, you may have additional privacy rights; we will honor them as the law requires. We will verify your request using your account email and never ask for your password.

## Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls and a database design that limits what each part of the service can read. No system is perfectly secure. Protect your password and tell us right away if you think your account was accessed without permission.

## Age and location

RevoCred is intended only for adults aged 18 or older in the United States. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change materially affects how we use your information, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. When the AI sharing notice changes, the app asks for your permission again.
