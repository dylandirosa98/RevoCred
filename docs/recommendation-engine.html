How a card becomes “next apply”

Application ranking only — engine v2.0.0-conservative. Not the Home 22–95 score, not S01–S09, not “which card to use at the store.”

1 Wallet Active cards only. Client IDs thrown out.
2 Catalog Public issuer snapshots. No demo, no custom.
3 Gates Student, owned, fee, credit, travel.
4 Delta $ Extra yearly rewards minus the new fee.
5 Score Those dollars, +20 goal, +10 travel.
6 Rank High score first. Slug breaks ties.
extra $ = Gross(wallet + card) − Gross(wallet) − fee
Gross = Σ round(monthly × 12 × best rate in the set × 1¢)
score = extra $ + 20 if goal matches + 10 if travel matches
drop if extra $ ≤ 0 · housing = 0 · food = min(dining, groceries) · bonuses ignored
